Today’s rule

How progress happens

The growth system.

Your best results come from training, progression, food, sleep, and recovery working together.

Progression formula

Earn small increases repeatedly.

01Choose a weight that leaves 2–3 clean repetitions possible.
02Add repetitions until every set reaches the top of its range.
03Increase the weight by the smallest available amount.
04Return to the lower end of the range and build again.
Effort

Challenging, controlled sets

Most working sets finish with about two clean repetitions left. Never sacrifice range or form just to complete a number. Do not test one-repetition maximums.

Food

Fuel training and growth

Eat regular balanced meals and snacks. Include protein foods, carbohydrates, fruits, vegetables, and dairy or another calcium-rich option. You do not need calorie counting or muscle-building supplements.

Sleep

Recover between sessions

Teenagers generally need 8–10 hours of sleep. Poor sleep, unusually low performance, or soreness that changes your movement means repeat or reduce the workout—not add more.

Soccer

Manage total workload

Monday and Wednesday are upper-focused because you also have soccer. After a hard game or Thursday practice, reduce Friday leg work by one set per exercise.

Expected changes

Track performance first

Expect strength, control, work capacity, and lifting technique to improve before appearance changes become obvious. Visible development varies with growth, genetics, meals, sleep, and consistency.

Guidance

Why the guardrails exist

Youth strength guidance emphasizes controlled technique, gradual increases, full comfortable motion, and avoiding maximal lifting. AAP guidance

Non-negotiables

Train hard without training recklessly.

These limits are part of the program, not optional extras.

Safety catches

Bench inside the protected range

Set the catches slightly below your normal chest position so they can stop the bar if a repetition fails. Test the empty bar setup first.

No failure requirement

Stop while form is still repeatable

Finish most sets with two clean repetitions left. A set ends if the bar path changes sharply, range shortens, or another repetition would require help.

Pain rule

Muscle effort is different from pain

Stop for sharp pain, joint pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath. Tell a parent or guardian if symptoms are concerning or persist.

Warm-up

Prepare, then perform

Do 5–10 minutes of easy movement and practice sets. For the first large exercise, complete 2–3 lighter sets before the listed working sets.

Recovery weeks

Every fourth week

The app removes one set from each exercise and asks you to use easier loads. This manages fatigue while keeping technique sharp.
