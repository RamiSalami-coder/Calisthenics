Rules that keep the plan working.

Schedule

Your week

  • Monday: lighter handstand/HSPU skill after soccer.
  • Tuesday: full-body strength A.
  • Wednesday: lighter pull skill after soccer.
  • Thursday: soccer only—no workout.
  • Friday: full-body strength B.
  • Saturday and Sunday: recovery or games.
Effort

Leave clean reps

Finish strength sets with about two good reps still possible. Skill sets end as soon as balance, speed, or control clearly drops.

Progression

Earn the next variation

Reach the top of the rep range on every set in two workouts, then reduce band help or choose the next variation. Change one thing at a time.

Soccer adjustment

Games come first

After a hard practice or game, cut leg sets in half. Never double the next workout to “make up” missed work.

Safety

Control before tricks

Use a clear wall and floor for handstands. Inspect bands and anchors. No free muscle-up attempts before five strict pull-ups and a safe bar setup.

Recovery

Thursday and weekends

Regular meals, sleep, and rest support training. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or joint pain and tell a parent or guardian.
